Physical intention in a weightless medium.

Most digital interfaces treat movement as mere decoration. We believe motion is an expression of mass, friction, and kinetic balance. When elements respect physical constraints—gravity, chain tension, and dampened inertia—the screen ceases to be an abstraction and becomes an enduring space you can feel.
